Solo Show Sugarglider Gallery, Sydney 9 July – 2 August 2025

'The
Crocodile'

The crocodile as ancient survivor, patient witness, cunning trickster — a new body of work circling a single, enduring creature.

Venue
Sugarglider Gallery
Sydney, NSW
Dates
9 July – 2 August 2025
Type
Solo Exhibition
Enquire

The crocodile is always there. Sometimes you see him, sometimes he sinks beneath the surface, but be assured he is watching. —

The Crocodile is many things.
You have your own Crocodile; just over there, lying in wait.
Even if you can't admit it to yourself, even if you close your eyes.

Meditating on and agonising over the persistence of chronic illness, generational curses, the rigidity of femininity, and substance abuse, Motormouth Comics presents a collection of terrible beasts and beloved airheads.

Special Thanks to: Fiona Macpherson, Vandida ARI and Sneaky Possum for organising and hosting. Thank you Nice China and Lonely Skeleton for opening night performances and Gusher and Problem Green for closing night performances.
