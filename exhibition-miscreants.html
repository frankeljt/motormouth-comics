Solo Show Prop Gallery, Sydney 31 January – 9 February 2025

'Miscreants'

A rogue's gallery of larrikins, troublemakers, and tenderly rendered portraits of defiance — characters who exist just outside the rules.

Venue
Prop Gallery
Sydney, NSW
Dates
31 January – 9 February 2025
Type
Solo Exhibition

/ˈmɪskriənt/ (noun): a person who has done something wrong or unlawful. A person behaving badly.

We are eels on the grass in the hot hot sun, slithering desperately for the lake, finding only dwindling pools. Our bodies tire, our once-soft skin blisters and bursts as gills choke on air. Do not despair; this is from no moral failing. You cannot find the lake because it was taken from you. Bit by bit it was drained, poisoned, filled in for a carpark, petrol station, new subdivision. The next puddle isn't far now, let's go there together. If we make it I'll buy you a drink.

Special thanks to: Prop Gallery, Grifter Brewing, Nat's dad for giving me his sofa, Kevin Mund for lending me his ute to deliver it, Lauren Mund for heavy lifting, and my friend Victoria from flying in from Melbourne to come see the show.
