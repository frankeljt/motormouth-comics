Group ShowJoin the Dots Gallery19–29 July 2023

'Phantas-
magoria'

A group exhibition exploring the spectral and the strange — visions from the edges of consciousness, rendered in ink, paint, and print.

Venue
Join the Dots Gallery
Dates
19–29 July 2023
Type
Group Exhibition

Kudos Offsite Presents: 'Phantasmagoria', a two person show featuring Lily Langley and Yvette Nemeth. Welcome to Phantasmagoria, the birth of a new world. Artists Yvette Nemeth and Lily Langley AKA Motormouth Comics present mythology melded with modern narratives to fabricate new myths of healing, trauma, and lived experience.

Special Thanks to: Carmen Schieb for organising, Kudos Gallery and Join the Dots ARI for hosting.
