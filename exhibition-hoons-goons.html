Solo Show Good Grief Studios, Hobart 20 January – 12 February 2023

'Hoons
& Goons'

Speed, swagger, and suburban mythology — a love letter to the larrikin in all its forms, rendered in ink and tyre marks.

Venue
Good Grief Studios
Hobart, TAS
Dates
20 January – 12 February 2023
Type
Solo Exhibition

The Worst Bunch of Girls You Ever Met

The characters present within the Hoons & Goons series are the social circle that is formed through the shared experience of evenings in the pub or parking at the 7/11 to show off your car. There is an inescapable love and hate that is created when you can't stop running into someone you know. These lot are the last standing goons left behind on a Thursday night when everyone else has gone home for the night, kicked out of one pub and on their way to another with longer opening hours. Whether or not they'll be let in has not begun to cross their minds, but it's crossing ours.

Special Thanks to: Thomas Friend and Jacob for organisation, installation, and Hobart recommendations, and also to Richard Langley for clearing up with me that we do not share a long-lost father. Thank you to Lucinda Langley for support, bankrolling, and all the laughs.
