Solo Show Goodspace Gallery, Sydney 25–27 January 2021

'Death in
Suburbia'

Lily Langley's debut solo exhibition — mortality, the uncanny, and the strange quiet of the cul-de-sac. A graduate student's reckoning with the neighbourhood she grew up in.

Venue
Goodspace Gallery
Sydney, NSW
Dates
25–27 January 2021
Type
Solo Exhibition
(Debut)
Context
BFA Honours year,
UNSW Art & Design, 2021
Recognition
Tim Olsen Drawing Prize
Finalist, 2021

'Death in Suburbia' is a series of collagraphs depicting death as a mundane eventuality as opposed to an ongoing threat. Death comes to us all, but until then he is our neighbour; just someone who lives down the road, who might be sometimes observed taking out his bins or mowing the lawn. The series takes us through a day in the domestic absurd life of death.

Special Thanks to: Drita Ajredin for her organisation and Goodspace Gallery for hosting. Thank you to Jake H.K Lucis for the poster design and helping me install and I guess always believing in me.
