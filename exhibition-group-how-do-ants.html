Group ShowGoodspace Gallery, Sydney29 November 2023

'How Do
Ants?'

Setting out to challenge the notion that the mundanity of life is ordinary and therefore uninteresting, 'How Do Ants?' takes a light-hearted stab at the often overlooked events of everyday life.

Venue
Goodspace Gallery
Sydney, NSW
Date
29 November 2023
Type
Group Exhibition

Setting out to challenge the notion that the mundanity of life is ordinary and therefore uninteresting, 'How Do Ants?' takes a light-hearted stab at the often overlooked events of everyday life. Through visual art, film and music, the show encourages attendees to pause and reflect on the absurdity of those little "wait, what?" life moments.

Featuring the art of:
Anthea Saraikin
Chelsea Cooper
Doctor Dollar Discounts
Motormouth Comics
Oli Main
